Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

In short: Tabulo does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any information about you. Everything you type stays on your device.

Who we are

Tabulo is a calculator app published by The Code Cipher (“we”). This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. Tabulo has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services. The Android version does not have permission to access the internet, and the app does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

To keep your work between sessions, Tabulo saves the following only on your device:

  • your calculation tabs, their names and notes,
  • the calculation history of each tab,
  • your settings (theme, angle mode, number format, keypad options).

We cannot see or access this information. You can delete history inside the app at any time, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

If your device’s own backup feature (for example Android backup or iCloud) is turned on, your operating system may include this app data in your device backup. That is handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies, not by Tabulo.

Clipboard

Tabulo reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste, and writes to it only when you choose to copy a result. Clipboard contents are never sent anywhere.

Sharing

If you use Share on a tab, the text of that calculation is passed to the app you choose (for example a messaging app). What happens next is governed by that app.

App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information when you download or update apps, under their own privacy policies. Tabulo does not receive any personal information from them.

Children

Tabulo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If Tabulo ever starts collecting any information, we will say so here before that version of the app is released.
