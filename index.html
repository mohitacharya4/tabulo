Tabulo

Many calculations. One calculator.

Tabulo is a simple calculator with tabs. Keep separate calculations open side by side, and use the result of one tab inside another. Change a number and everything linked to it updates.

  • Multiple named tabs, each with its own saved history
  • Linked results between tabs
  • Exact decimal math: 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3
  • Edit anywhere in your calculation, with undo and redo
  • Scientific functions, dark mode, one-handed keypad
  • No ads, no account, no tracking, works fully offline

Quick help

How do I add or switch tabs?
Tap ＋ in the tab bar to add a tab. Swipe the display left or right, or tap a tab, to switch.
How do I use one tab’s result in another?
Tap Tab above the keypad and pick a tab. Its result is inserted as a linked chip and stays up to date.
How do I rename, share or close a tab?
Tap the tab you are on (or long-press any tab) to open its options.
How does % work?
200 + 10% = 220, 200 − 10% = 180, and 10% × 200 = 20.
I cleared something by mistake.
Tap ↶ (undo). Clearing can always be undone.
